# 28 — Focused Journey and Less Repeated Management

Authorized player feedback, 2026-10-13: the connected loop is more interesting,
but opening several functions together obscures their value and demands too much
management. Extends 24, 26 and 27; dependencies 07, 13, 14, 20–22.
This is the implementation contract for the current iteration.

## One principal system per early rank

New journeys introduce one principal growth system at a time:

| Rank | Principal discovery | Change to play |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Junior | Team and cash upgrades | Turn manual testing into steady production |
| Middle | Projects | Use that production to deliver a first client certificate |
| Senior | Main CI/CD | Allocate capacity to grow permanent autotest capital |
| Lead | Product roles | Choose income from installed capital, faster growth or saved insights |
| Head | Studio research | Invest accumulated insights in permanent development |
| Director / first prestige | Architecture | Rebuild the earlier pipeline through spatial configurations |
| All blueprints | Network | Use a self-growing generator chain and connected Agents |

Automatic reporting remains a Middle entitlement that removes mastered paperwork.
New crew and upgrades expand the existing workspace, not additional major systems.
Projects still earn and retain insights before research opens; those savings later
give the Studio an immediate purpose. Do not force a minimum residence time.

For the focused journey, the first unowned tool requires at least Lead in addition
to its existing certificate/current-rank requirements. Other tool requirements,
prices, roles and owned effects stay unchanged. Research and specialist assignment
require best rank Head; prerequisite trees still apply. Lead's promotion to Head
demonstrates one owned tool as well as one trial reward. This is one actual use of
the new system, not a collection checklist. Owned tools persist and satisfy the
demonstration on repeat careers. Existing old-journey objectives remain supported.

Keep descriptions and promotion announcements aligned with that sequence. Before
research is accessible, project completion does not promise a specialist waiting
in an inaccessible Studio. Preserve earned knowledge without exposing a map of
all later layers. Only the nearest principal discovery should be announced.

## Quiet optional overviews

Office, Career history, Resources and Achievements are overviews, not simultaneous
new growth-layer announcements. Focused journeys place their unlocked entries in
an expandable Overviews group; Workspace, Projects, CI/CD, Products, Studio and
Settings remain direct navigation. Only eligible overview actions enter the DOM
when the group is opened. Resources also keep their existing direct shortcut.
Old journeys retain their familiar navigation. Selecting an overview does not
change gameplay unlocks or hide any owned assets.
Focused journeys keep the optional Workspace contract panel collapsed until opened.
The resource overview remains accessible after the first earned insights; before
their investment systems open, it shows saved knowledge without an inaccessible
Studio shortcut. Project cards reveal tool and specialist benefits only after
the corresponding introduction, rather than advertising several future systems.

## Automate work after its first demonstration

The Budget trial additionally opens optional automatic main-core allocation.
It maximizes sustainable throughput after enabling, new cores, capacity-changing
purchases and load. Default off. Trials keep independent, manual allocations.
Keep the existing architecture.autoBalance field for save compatibility: it now
represents main-pipeline management and is valid after Budget even before a rack.
All rack modules, layouts, power, blueprints and autoCores remain inert until their
existing prerequisites. The Flow blueprint still opens the manager module; it no
longer presents already available balancing as a new discovery.

Provide an explicit bulk choice for already unlocked hire, cash-upgrade and project
policies. Show the included actions and money spending before the choice. It sets
only eligible policies; subsequent unlocks need another player choice. Separate
automatic promotion, insight-spending Auto Studio, core/network purchases and
reboots retain their individual controls. No automatic opt-in or retroactive work.
The existing five-second cadence and validated purchase actions stay unchanged.

## Persistence and rollout

Add journeyModel 1/2 to the existing v4 save. Fresh games use 2. Missing/unknown
markers on saves that reached Middle or have a previous career use 1, retaining
their earlier discovery, owned assets and objectives. Earlier unopened careers
may use 2 because none of the shifted systems were reached. Persist the explicit
model on export/checkpoint; valid markers round-trip. No career reset.

Owned tools are normalized under the journey's introduction rank; old model 1
retains the historical Senior ownership rule. Their active effects retain the
original tool rank, including on repeat careers. Research normalization uses
the appropriate journey eligibility without changing project, insight or certificate
balances. Defaults never enable balancing or purchase policies.

## Verification

Prove sequential new-journey layer announcements and hidden controls through
real promotion/project/trial actions. Validate locked intents as well as UI absence,
old model 1 and new model 2 saves, malformed markers, repeat careers, early Budget
balancing, independent trial allocation, bulk opt-in and offline settlement/parity.
Measure clicks and discoveries in a first and repeat career. Check desktop/mobile,
resource access and the player's original save separately. Run full checks, builds
and CI; keep the goal active until the focused cycle and its remaining requirements
are actually verified. Prior player feedback supports the connected direction,
not a claim that this new iteration has already been accepted.
