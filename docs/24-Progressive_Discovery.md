# 24 — Progressive Discovery and Layer Identity

Status: authorized 2026-10-12. Extends 16–23. Owns presentation of already defined
unlocks and the visual identity of each layer. Dependencies: 03, 07, 13, 16–23.
This supersedes the full future-system previews/trails in 21–23. No economy,
production, costs, action requirements or save schema changes.

## Discovery contract

Start with Workspace and Settings only. Hidden tabs are absent from navigation and
cannot be opened through shortcuts. One nearby goal may identify the next milestone;
it must not expose the full future systems map. Newly unlocked tabs appear with a
short announcement and are available immediately. Existing saves reveal everything
already reached; loading a save never requires repeating its milestones.

| View / feature | Reveal condition |
| --- | --- |
| Workspace, Settings | Always |
| Office, Projects, Career | Best rank at least Middle QA |
| Studio | Middle QA plus earned insights, research or recruited specialist |
| Products | A tool's first certificate and its best-rank requirement, or owned tool |
| Achievements | First earned badge; display earned badges only |
| Prestige, permanent experience | Best rank at least Director, or existing prestige |
| CI/CD | First career prestige |
| Architecture subtab | All three trial rewards |
| Network subtab | All four blueprint rewards |

Keep revealed tabs through ordinary career prestige using existing permanent
progress (bestStage, certificates, lifetimeInsights, research, careers and rewards).
No new unlock flags. If importing a younger save or resetting closes the current
view, render Workspace immediately. Hidden controls must also be absent from
keyboard navigation and the accessibility tree.

Inside each layer, reveal content at its existing prerequisites: research after its
prerequisite, specialists after recruiting, products after certificate/best rank,
projects after initial rank and preceding first certificate, dispatcher after its
rank and three contracts, trials after 100 releases, career policies after their
reward, rack modules and policies after their blueprint. Show only completed and
the current blueprint. Generator cards and chain nodes appear at their best-run
thresholds. Reboot controls appear at the first 2000 best-run computations;
protocol choices appear after earning the first protocol; autobuy switch appears
after purchasing its protocol. Affordability alone never hides revealed content.

Career history lists ranks already reached and the next rank only. Office floorplan
lists reached locations only. Do not show total undiscovered collections or future
crew cards. Workspace progression before CI/CD focuses on the next rank/project,
and at Director points to the first prestige. After CI/CD, one nearest discovery
continues to guide the player using existing authoritative selectors.

## Visual identity

Keep the dark compact dashboard, readable text, stable layout and familiar controls.
Give each active layer a distinct accent, tinted surface and header marker:
Workspace mint, Office warm amber, Projects blue, Products rose, Studio violet,
Career gold, Pipeline cyan, Architecture orange, Network periwinkle.
Apply accents to navigation, headings, meters, primary actions and selected states.
Layer names/icons remain present so colour is never the only indicator. Scope CSS
tokens to the application/layer; semantic warning and resource colours remain clear.
Support 320px and desktop, visible keyboard focus and reduced motion.

## Acceptance

Verify fresh-game absence of all future tabs and controls; rank, insight, tool,
prestige, trial, blueprint, generator and protocol reveal transitions; retained
visibility after prestige/reload; importing a younger save from an open late layer;
old-save compatibility. Check contrasting layer themes visually on desktop/mobile.
Run full validation and portable build, preserve the user's actual save and deliver
the reviewed result directly onto main. Track four iterations in TODO.md and report
completed/remaining items at the end of each iteration.
